# The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

The 50/30/20 rule is a baseline budgeting framework for splitting after-tax income:

- **50% Needs**: rent, utilities, groceries, minimum debt payments, insurance.
- **30% Wants**: dining out, subscriptions, entertainment, non-essential shopping.
- **20% Savings & Debt Payoff**: emergency fund, retirement contributions, extra
  payments above the minimum on any debt.

## When it doesn't fit
In high cost-of-living cities, "needs" often exceeds 50% of income for renters.
In that case, treat 20% savings as the floor to protect, not the number to cut
first — trim "wants" before trimming savings.

## Common mistake
Classifying subscriptions (streaming, food delivery apps, unused gym
memberships) as "needs." They belong in the 30% Wants bucket and are usually
the fastest place to find extra savings.
