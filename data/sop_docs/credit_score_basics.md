# Credit Score Basics

## The five factors (approximate weight, FICO model)
1. **Payment history (35%)** — on-time payments matter more than anything else.
2. **Credit utilization (30%)** — balance divided by credit limit, across all
   revolving accounts. Keeping this under 30%, ideally under 10%, helps most.
3. **Length of credit history (15%)** — average age of accounts; this is why
   closing your oldest card can hurt your score.
4. **Credit mix (10%)** — a mix of revolving (cards) and installment (loans).
5. **New credit (10%)** — hard inquiries from new applications, short-term dip.

## Fastest realistic ways to improve a score
- Pay down revolving balances before the statement closing date, not just the
  due date — utilization is usually reported at statement close.
- Never miss a payment; set autopay for at least the minimum.
- Don't close old, no-fee cards even if unused — it shortens average account
  age and reduces total available credit.

## Myths
Checking your own score is a soft inquiry and does not hurt it. Carrying a
small balance month-to-month does not help your score.
