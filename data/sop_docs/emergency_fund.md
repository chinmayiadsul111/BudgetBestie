# Building an Emergency Fund

## Target size
3–6 months of essential expenses (rent, utilities, groceries, minimum debt
payments, insurance) — not 3–6 months of your full current spending.

## Where to keep it
A high-yield savings account, separate from your checking account, so it's
liquid but not sitting next to your everyday spending money.

## Build order when income is tight
1. $500–$1,000 starter buffer first, even before aggressively paying down
   low-interest debt — this prevents new debt from a single car repair or
   medical bill.
2. Then split extra cash flow between high-interest debt payoff and growing
   the fund toward the full 3–6 month target.

## What counts as an emergency
Job loss, medical bills, essential car/home repairs. Not a sale on something
you wanted, a friend's destination wedding, or a predictable annual expense
(that belongs in a sinking fund, not the emergency fund).
